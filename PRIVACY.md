# Privacy policy — Obsidian Research

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Obsidian Research is a desktop-only plugin package. It does not operate a developer-owned server or collect telemetry. Its bundled scripts do not send Obsidian notes, vault contents, or personal details to the plugin developer.

When you ask Codex to research a topic, your prompt, source pages, and draft note may be processed by the Codex environment and the web tools available in your session under their own terms and privacy policies. The URL crawler sends HTTP requests directly from your computer to the sites you choose; those sites may receive your IP address, request time, URL, and the plugin's user-agent. The crawler does not send cookies or authentication headers.

The plugin stores the finished Markdown note, any permitted images you choose to download, image attribution sidecars, and per-vault preferences only in the Obsidian vault you select. Temporary research files are created only as part of the Codex task and should be removed after use. The plugin developer has no access to those files and does not retain copies. You can remove created files using your file manager or Obsidian.

The plugin needs a topic or URL and a vault path to complete a request. Do not provide credentials, payment information, government identifiers, or private data you do not want processed by Codex or the sites involved.

For support or privacy questions, open an issue in this repository.
